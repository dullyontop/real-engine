# RE0503: Script asked for something an entity does not have

A script called a runtime service for an entity that lacks what the service
needs: `physics.move_character` on an entity without a `CharacterController`, an
impulse on an entity without a body, `ui` text on an entity without `HudText`,
or regeneration of an id that is not a procedural source.

## Example

```text
error[RE0503]: entity 4294967301 has no character (add a CharacterController)
```

## How to fix it

Add the missing component to the entity in its scene or prefab, or call the
service on the right entity.
