# RE0509: Screenshot differs from its golden image

A replay's screenshot check captured a frame that differs from its golden image
by more than the tolerance in `real.toml [verify]` (`golden_mean_tolerance`, the
mean absolute difference per channel, and `golden_max_outliers`, the share of
pixels off by more than 32 levels).

## Example

```text
error[RE0509]: tests/goldens/vulkan/island_600.png: mean difference 6.20 (tolerance 2.00)
```

## How to fix it

Look at the written capture next to the golden. If the change is intended,
replace the golden with the new capture; goldens are kept per backend.
