# RE0570: Scene load failed

A scene that `scene.switch` or `scene.load` asked for could not be loaded:
its file cannot be read or does not parse, or its entities do not load
beside what stays in the world (an id that a remaining entity already has, a
parent or an entity reference that does not exist, an unknown component or a
field value of the wrong type). The scene's own diagnostic is attached as a
related note when there is one, and the `SceneLoad` that asked ends with its
`error` set.

Nothing changed: a switch despawns the scenes it leaves only after the new
scene has passed its checks. A scene added with `scene.load` may not hold a
streamed entity, since streaming belongs to the main scene.

## Example

```text
error[RE0570]: the scene "harbour" (scenes/harbour.scene.json) does not load
  --> scenes/harbour.scene.json
  note[RE1201]: an entity with id "sun" already exists
```

## How to fix it

Fix the problem the related diagnostic names; `real verify` checks every
scene file. Two scenes loaded together must not share ids: give the entities
of a scene that `scene.load` adds ids of their own, or switch to it instead.
