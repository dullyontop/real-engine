# RE0520: Asset not available

A component names a mesh, material or animation clip that cannot be loaded, so
the entity is drawn without it (or not drawn at all, for a mesh). It is reported
once per asset; the related diagnostic, when there is one, is the asset loader's
own error.

## Example

```text
error[RE0520]: `builtin:teapot`: no such built-in mesh
  --> builtin:teapot
  = help: built-in meshes are builtin:cube, builtin:sphere, builtin:plane and builtin:capsule
```

## How to fix it

Check the path in `MeshRef.asset`, `MaterialRef.asset` or `AnimPlayer.clip`
against the files under `assets/` and `materials/`. A `derived:` mesh exists only
after its procedural source has generated it.
