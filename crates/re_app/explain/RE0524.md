# RE0524: Character not re-grounded

The terrain under a character was regenerated during play, and a downward ray
at the character's position found no ground, so the character was left where
it was (it may fall).

## Example

```text
warning[RE0524]: no ground below character "player" after the terrain changed
```

## How to fix it

Keep characters over the terrain the graph generates (inside its size), or
move the character in a script after `proc.regenerate`.
