# RE0529: Ragdoll unavailable

A `Ragdoll` component could not be turned into a physics ragdoll on its skinned mesh
(ADR-0040). The entity shows its animation without a ragdoll. The problem is reported
once, until the component, the skin or the entity's scale changes.

The ragdoll is generated on the skin's skeleton: one capsule per chosen joint along its
bone, humanoid joint limits by joint name, and the total mass split by volume.

## How to fix it

- With an empty `joints` list, the engine looks for a humanoid's hips, spine, head, upper
  arms, forearms, thighs and shins by name (Blender, Mixamo and Roblox R15 spellings). If
  your joints are named otherwise, list the joints that get parts in `Ragdoll.joints`.
- Every listed joint must be a joint of the skin, listed once, and one of them must be
  above all the others (usually the hips).
- `total_mass` must be positive.
- The entity's world scale must be the same on all three axes.
- An entity has a ragdoll instead of a `RigidBody`; remove the body (`RE5005` otherwise).

Problems found by physics itself, such as limits out of range, are `RE5019`.
