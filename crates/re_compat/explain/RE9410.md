# RE9410: Surface or viewport cannot be drawn as asked

A `SurfaceGui` is drawn on a face of a part: its `Adornee`, or the part it
is parented to when `Adornee` is empty. This warning says one of two things,
once per `SurfaceGui`:

- It has no part to sit on (no `Adornee`, and its parent is not a part), so
  it is not drawn.
- Its canvas is larger than the 2048 pixels a side a surface texture holds,
  so the whole canvas is drawn scaled down to fit and looks blurrier. The
  layout (`AbsoluteSize` and the rest) still uses the full canvas.

## Example

```text
warning[RE9410]: SurfaceGui Workspace.Sign.SurfaceGui's canvas of 4000x1000 pixels is drawn scaled down to fit a 2048-pixel texture
```

## How to fix it

Set `Adornee` to a part, or parent the `SurfaceGui` to one. For a large
canvas, use `SizingMode = PixelsPerStud` with a lower `PixelsPerStud`, or a
smaller `CanvasSize`.
