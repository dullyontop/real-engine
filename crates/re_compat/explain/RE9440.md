# RE9440: Bones and skin joints do not match

A `MeshPart` whose `MeshId` resolves to a glTF model with a skin is posed by
the `Bone` instances under it: each bone drives the skin joint with the same
name, through its `Transform`. This warning means the two lists differ. A
bone names no joint of the skin, so nothing it does shows, or a joint of the
skin has no bone, so that joint stays in its rest pose. It is reported once
per part, naming the first few names on each side.

## Example

```text
warning[RE9440]: Workspace.Robot.Body: the bones [Tail] name no joint of the skin `Armature`, and the joints [Head, Neck] have no bone; those joints stay at rest
```

## How to fix it

Rename the bones to match the joint names in the model file (open the file
in a modelling tool to see them), add a bone for each joint that should
move, or remove bones the model does not have. Bones may sit anywhere under
the part, as in an imported rig where each bone is a child of its parent
bone.
