# RE9351: Effect budget exceeded

More particles, beam segments or trail points were alive in one frame than
the effect budget allows. The oldest particles and the farthest effects are
dropped for that frame so the frame time stays bounded. The count of what was
dropped is reported in the frame's performance report.

## Example

```text
warning[RE9351]: 41200 particles this frame, over the budget of 20000; 21200 were not drawn
```

## How to fix it

Lower the emitters' `Rate` or `Lifetime`, disable emitters that are far from
the camera, or raise the budget in `real.toml` (`[compat] effect_budget`).
