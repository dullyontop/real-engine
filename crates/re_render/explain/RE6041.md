# RE6041: Undrawable skinned item

A `SkinnedDrawItem` in the render snapshot could not be drawn, and was
skipped. These checks run when the renderer poses skins on the GPU; without
GPU skinning (`RE6040`) every skinned item is drawn in its bind pose and none
is checked. One of these is true:

- its mesh was uploaded with `Renderer::upload_mesh`, not
  `Renderer::upload_skinned_mesh`, so it has no joints or weights;
- its palette range (`palette .. palette + joints`) runs past the end of the
  snapshot's `joint_palette`;
- `joints` is smaller than the highest joint index the mesh's weights use, plus
  one;
- the joint palette is larger than the renderer can bind (more than 2^24
  matrices, or more than the device's storage buffer binding limit).

## Example

```text
warning[RE6041]: skinned item of entity 12 uses palette 40..60, but the joint palette has 48 matrices
```

## How to fix it

Upload skinned meshes with `upload_skinned_mesh`, and give each item the run
of `joint_palette` its skin's palette was written to. `re_app` builds both
from `SkinnedMesh` components; a hand-built snapshot must keep them in step.
