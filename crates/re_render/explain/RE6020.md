# RE6020: GPU readback failed

Copying a texture back to the CPU failed: the GPU did not finish within 60
seconds, the buffer could not be mapped, or the texture format or aspect cannot
be copied. Depth textures cannot be read back on the GL backend.

## Example

```text
error[RE6020]: waiting for the GPU failed: Waiting for the GPU timed out
```

## How to fix it

A timeout usually means a driver problem or a device lost after an earlier
`RE6001` error; look for that first. For depth readback, use the Vulkan backend.
