# RE6011: Stale texture handle

A texture handle was used after the texture was released, or it was never
issued by this renderer. This happens when creating a material that names such a
texture, or when releasing a texture twice.

## Example

```text
error[RE6011]: the texture handle 0:2 was released or never issued
```

## How to fix it

Upload the texture again with `Renderer::upload_texture` and use the new handle.
A material keeps its textures alive on the GPU, so releasing a texture after
creating the material is safe.
