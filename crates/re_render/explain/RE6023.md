# RE6023: Compared images differ in size

Two images compared with `imgdiff` (or a golden-image test) have different
sizes, so they cannot be compared pixel by pixel.

## Example

```text
error[RE6023]: the images have different sizes: 320x240 and 640x480
```

## How to fix it

Render at the golden image's size, or regenerate the golden with
`RE_UPDATE_GOLDENS=1` after checking that the new size is intended.
