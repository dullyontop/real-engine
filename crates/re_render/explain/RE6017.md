# RE6017: Too many lights

A frame has more lights than the forward pass draws. It draws one directional
light (the first that casts shadows, otherwise the first), the eight point
lights nearest the camera and the eight spot lights nearest the camera. The
others are ignored for that frame.

## Example

```text
warning[RE6017]: 11 point lights in the frame; only the 8 nearest the camera are drawn
```

## How to fix it

Use one sun, and keep at most eight point and eight spot lights near the
camera: merge lights
that are close together, or turn off the ones far from the action.
