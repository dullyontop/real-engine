# RE7060: Malformed glTF skin

An error: a skin in a glTF file, or the skin weights of one of its meshes,
cannot be imported (ADR-0039). The file does not load. The message names the
skin or mesh and the problem:

- a skin with no joints, with a joint listed twice, or with more than 65,535 joints;
- inverse bind matrices that are not `MAT4` floats, or fewer of them than joints;
- `JOINTS_n` without `WEIGHTS_n` (or the reverse), with the wrong type, or with
  a different length from the mesh's positions;
- a vertex weighted to a joint index the skin does not have.

## Example

```text
error[RE7060]: skin `Armature`: it has 52 joints but 50 inverse bind matrices
  --> assets/models/hero.glb
```

## How to fix it

Re-export the model from the modelling tool with skinning enabled, and check it
with the Khronos glTF validator. Joints must be `VEC4` unsigned bytes or shorts;
weights `VEC4` floats or normalized unsigned bytes or shorts.
