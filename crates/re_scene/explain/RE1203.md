# RE1203: Hierarchy cycle

A reparent would put an entity under itself or under one of its own
descendants, which would make the hierarchy a cycle.

## Example

```text
error[RE1203]: cannot parent "level" under "crate": "crate" is "level" or one of its descendants
  --> entity `level`
```

## How to fix it

Choose a parent outside the entity's subtree, or move the descendant out first.
