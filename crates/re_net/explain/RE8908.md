# RE8908: Message cannot be sent

A message was sent on a connection that is not open, or it is larger than
its channel allows: 1,170 bytes on the unreliable channel (one datagram), or
1 MiB on the reliable channel.

## Example

```text
error[RE8908]: an unreliable message of 2048 bytes is larger than the 1170 bytes the channel allows
```

## How to fix it

Send large data on the reliable channel, or split it into smaller messages.
Check that the connection is still open before sending; a closed connection
is reported once as RE8906 or RE8907.
