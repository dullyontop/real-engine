# RE8906: Connection timed out

A connection received nothing from the other end for the whole timeout (10 s
unless `[net] timeout_ms` says otherwise), or the other end stopped
acknowledging what it was sent until more than 4 MiB waited. The other
process exited, its machine left the network, or the network between them
went down.

## Example

```text
error[RE8906]: the server at 192.168.1.20:47800 sent nothing for 10 s
```

## How to fix it

Check that the other process is still running and that the network between
the machines works. A client can connect again once the server is reachable.
