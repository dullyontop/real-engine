# RE2003: Structural edit of a generated entity

Entities spawned by a procedural source (ids `<source id>/<key>`) are owned by
their generator. Field edits are allowed and are kept as overrides, and deleting
one records a deletion override, but reparenting, renaming, adding or removing
components, and giving it authored children are refused: the next regeneration
would undo them.

## Example

```text
error[RE2003]: "yard/pillar_3" is generated: change its source's generator parameters instead
```

## How to fix it

Change the source's generator parameters (its `Procedural` component or the
parameter component its generator reads), or move the edit to an authored entity.
