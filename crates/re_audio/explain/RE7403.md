# RE7403: Too many sounds playing

A sound was not started because the audio mixer is full. Each spatial sound
uses one mixer track, and there are 128; finished sounds free their track.

## Example

```text
warning[RE7403]: a sound was not started: Could not add a resource because the maximum capacity for that resource has been reached
```

## How to fix it

Play fewer sounds at once: stop looping sources that are far from the
listener, and avoid starting a one-shot sound every frame.
