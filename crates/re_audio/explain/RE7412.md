# RE7412: Invalid spatial sound settings

An entity's spatial sound settings (the `rolloff`, `min_distance`,
`max_distance` and `doppler` fields of its `AudioSource`) cannot apply. The
distances are metres and must be finite, `min_distance` 0 or more and
`max_distance` above it; the `inverse` rolloff, whose level halves with each
doubling of `min_distance`, needs a `min_distance` above 0. `doppler` must be a
finite number of 0 or more: 0 is none, 1 is physical, and more exaggerates.
The sound keeps the settings it had.

## Example

```text
error[RE7412]: spatial sound settings refused: max_distance 1 is not a finite number above min_distance 5
```

## How to fix it

Set `max_distance` above `min_distance`, for example `min_distance = 2` and
`max_distance = 40` for a sound heard across a room. Use the `off` rolloff for
a sound that only pans, at the same volume at any distance.
