# RE7408: Unknown audio bus or snapshot

A sound, a command or a snapshot names a bus, a send or a snapshot that the
audio graph does not have. A sound on an unknown bus plays on `default_bus`.
A volume, effect or snapshot command that names something unknown does nothing.
This warning is given once per name.

## Example

```text
warning[RE7408]: a sound names audio bus `fottsteps`, which the graph does not have
```

## How to fix it

Use a name declared under `[audio.buses]`, `[audio.sends]` or
`[audio.snapshots]` in `real.toml`. A project without an `[audio]` section has
the buses `music`, `sfx` and `ui`. Sounds play on buses, not on sends: route a
bus to a send with its `sends` table.
