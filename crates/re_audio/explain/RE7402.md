# RE7402: Audio clip cannot be decoded

The clip file exists but could not be read or decoded: it is damaged, or
not what its name says. The engine plays WAV files (PCM at 8, 16, 24 or 32
bit, or 32-bit float) and Ogg Vorbis files, mono or stereo. A file of another
kind is `RE7409`.

`real build` decodes every Ogg file it packs once, and refuses a damaged one
with this code.

## Example

```text
error[RE7402]: cannot decode audio clip `assets/music/theme.wav`: ...
  --> assets/music/theme.wav
```

## How to fix it

Re-export the file, or convert it to a mono or stereo WAV or Ogg Vorbis file,
for example with `ffmpeg -i theme.flac -c:a pcm_s16le theme.wav` or
`ffmpeg -i theme.flac -c:a libvorbis theme.ogg`.
