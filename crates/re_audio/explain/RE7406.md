# RE7406: Invalid audio effect

An effect in the `[audio]` section of `real.toml`, in a mixer snapshot, or in a
script's `audio.set_effect` call has a value out of range, or names a parameter
the effect does not have. When the graph is built, the effect is dropped and
the rest of its bus still plays. It keeps its index, so later effects keep
theirs. A command with a bad value leaves the effect as it was.

The ranges are:

- `reverb`: `feedback`, `damping`, `stereo_width` and `mix` from 0 to 1.
- `filter`: `cutoff_hz` from 10 to 20,000, and `resonance` and `mix` from 0 to 1.
- `delay`: `seconds` from 0.001 to 10, `feedback_db` 0 or less, and `mix` from 0 to 1.
- `compressor`: `ratio` 1 or more, `attack_ms` and `release_ms` from 0 to
  10,000, and `mix` from 0 to 1.
- `eq`: `hz` from 10 to 20,000, and `q` above 0.
- `distortion`: `mix` from 0 to 1.

Every value must be finite. An effect's kind cannot change, so a filter has no
`feedback_db`. A delay's `seconds` is fixed once its bus is built. A parameter
change or a mixer snapshot tweens over 0 to 3,600 seconds (an hour); a longer,
negative or non-finite length is refused with this code.

## Example

```text
error[RE7406]: effect 0 (filter) on `footsteps` is dropped: cutoff_hz 5 is not between 10 and 20000 Hz
```

## How to fix it

Bring the value into its range. To change an effect's kind, edit `real.toml`.
The graph is rebuilt when the project reloads.
