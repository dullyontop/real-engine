# RE7407: Invalid audio bus graph

The buses and sends in the `[audio]` section of `real.toml` do not form a
graph. The causes are:

- a bus's `parent` is not a bus;
- a bus sends to something that is not a send;
- parents form a cycle;
- a name is both a bus and a send;
- `default_bus` is not a bus;
- there are more than 15 sends;
- a volume is not finite;
- a snapshot is called `default`, which is reserved for the graph's own values.

The engine uses the default graph instead: the buses `music`, `sfx` and `ui`
under the main track, with no effects. A typo therefore never silences a game.

## Example

```text
error[RE7407]: bus `footsteps` has parent `sxf`, which is not a bus
```

## How to fix it

Correct the name. A bus with no `parent` sits under the main track.
