# RE7604: Malformed keyframe clip

A keyframe clip cannot be played because some of its data is out of range.
A keyframe clip needs:

- keyframe and marker times that are finite and not negative (keyframes may
  be given in any order; they are sorted by time);
- joint keys with finite translations, finite rotations that are not all
  zero (they are normalised), and weights that are finite and not negative.

## Example

```text
error[RE7604]: keyframe 2 (`Swing`) has time -0.5; times must be finite and >= 0
```

## How to fix it

Fix the clip in the tool that made it, or correct the values when building
the clip by hand.
