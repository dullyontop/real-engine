# RE5007: Invalid body settings

Jolt refused a body's settings or a change to it. The message names the value.

## How to fix it

- A dynamic body's mass must be between 0.001 kg and 1,000,000 kg.
- Friction and damping must be non-negative and finite; restitution between 0 and 1.
- Rotations must be non-zero and finite (they are normalised for you).
- Velocities must be finite and below 500 m/s.
- A body held by a constraint cannot change its shape; remove the constraint first.
