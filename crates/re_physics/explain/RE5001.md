# RE5001: The physics world could not be created

`re_physics::Physics::new` passed the `PhysicsConfig` to Jolt, and Jolt refused it.
The message says which value was out of range.

## Example

```text
error[RE5001]: the physics world could not be created: invalid world value: max_bodies must be between 1 and 2^23
  = hint: check the PhysicsConfig limits and gravity
```

## How to fix it

- `max_bodies` must be between 1 and 8,388,608; `max_body_pairs` and
  `max_contact_constraints` must be at least 1 (contact constraints at most 1,048,576).
- `gravity` must be finite.
- The worker pool must have at most 64 threads.
