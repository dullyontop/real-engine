# RE5013: Mesh triangles dropped

A triangle-mesh collider contained degenerate triangles (zero area, or repeated
vertices), and Jolt left them out. The rest of the mesh works; the dropped triangles
do not collide.

## Example

```text
warning[RE5013]: the triangle mesh lost 6 of 1280 triangles (degenerate or duplicate)
  --> entity `#12`
  = hint: remove zero-area triangles from the mesh
```

## How to fix it

- Remove zero-area and duplicate triangles from the mesh before using it as a
  collider. Procedural meshes often produce them at seams and poles.
