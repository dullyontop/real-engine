# RE5012: Physics state could not be restored

`Physics::restore_state` was given a snapshot it cannot apply. A snapshot restores only
into the world that saved it, and only while that world's structure is unchanged.

## How to fix it

- Restore before adding or removing bodies, characters or constraints, and before
  replacing a body's shape.
- Restore before the simulation origin moves (`Physics::set_origin`, which world
  streaming calls as the focus travels): a snapshot from before a move does not
  restore after it.
- Save a new snapshot after changing the world's structure.
- To carry motion into another world, such as a fresh `Physics` built from a copy of
  the scene, use `Physics::motion_state` on the source and `Physics::apply_motion_state`
  on the copy. They match bodies and characters by entity.
