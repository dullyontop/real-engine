# RE5005: Entity already has a body

A body, character or constraint was added for an entity that already has one. Each
entity owns at most one body or character, and at most one constraint.

## Example

```text
error[RE5005]: entity #12 already has a body, character or ragdoll
  --> entity `#12`
  = hint: remove the existing body first, or send SetShape to change its shape
```

## How to fix it

- To change the shape of an existing body, send `SetShape` instead of adding a new
  body.
- To change a body's kind, remove the body and add it again.
- Put a second constraint on a child entity of its own.
