# RE5006: Position outside the world limits

A body, character or kinematic target was placed outside the area physics can
simulate. Positions are single-precision floats, so Jolt only accepts positions within
5 km of the simulation origin on every axis; precision degrades well before that.

With world streaming the simulation origin follows the focus (ADR-0042). While it is
moved, this is a warning: a body added out of reach waits and joins once the origin
comes within 5 km of it, and a new position for a body or character out of reach is
ignored (reported once per origin) while it stays where it is.

## How to fix it

- Keep simulated objects within a few kilometres of the origin.
- For larger worlds, move the origin with the player (re-centre the world) instead of
  letting coordinates grow.
- Check for a position that became infinite or NaN through a division by zero.
