# RE5017: Invalid assembly of parts

An assembly (a rigid body built from parts) could not be created or changed.
Either its part list was empty, named one part twice, gave a part a collision
group of 32 or more, or held a size or pose that is not a finite number; or
the physics engine refused the combined shape.

## How to fix it

- Give every assembly at least one part, and each part a unique entity.
- Keep part sizes and poses finite. Sizes below 0.001 are raised to 0.001.
- Register at most 32 collision groups.
