# RE5018: Invalid vehicle

A vehicle could not be created or driven (ADR-0040). The message names the problem, and
the command was dropped: nothing was created or changed.

## Example

```text
error[RE5018]: vehicle #30: its chassis is a Kinematic body; it must be dynamic
  --> entity `#30`
  = hint: a vehicle needs a dynamic body on the same entity, at least one wheel (a motorcycle exactly two) and positive sizes, torques and gear ratios
```

## How to fix it

- The vehicle's entity needs a **dynamic** body first: the chassis. Give it a `RigidBody`
  with `kind = "Dynamic"` and a `Collider`.
- A vehicle needs at least one wheel and at least one driven wheel. A motorcycle needs
  exactly two wheels, front first, apart along the chassis' forward axis (+Z).
- Driven wheels with the same local `z` (within 1 cm) form an axle, and an axle has at most
  two wheels.
- Sizes, suspension lengths, torques, rpm and gear ratios must be positive and finite;
  forward gears positive and reverse gears negative; `suspension_max` at least
  `suspension_min`; a sphere tester's radius smaller than every wheel's
  `suspension_max + radius`.
- Driver input: `throttle` and `steer` in [-1, 1], `brake` and `handbrake` in [0, 1].
- An entity has at most one vehicle; remove it before adding another.
