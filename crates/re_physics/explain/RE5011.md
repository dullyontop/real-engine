# RE5011: Physics step dropped contacts

A physics step ran out of room in one of its fixed-size buffers, so some contacts were
ignored for that step. Objects may pass through each other or jitter.

## How to fix it

- Raise `PhysicsConfig::max_body_pairs` and `PhysicsConfig::max_contact_constraints`.
- Look for many bodies piled in one place, or large bodies overlapping many others.
