# RE5002: Invalid collision shape

A `Collider` (or a `SetShape` command) described a shape whose numbers make no sense,
or Jolt refused to build it.

## How to fix it

- Box half extents, sphere and capsule radii, and heightfield spacing must be positive
  and finite. A capsule's half height may be 0 (it is then a sphere).
- A convex hull needs at least 4 points that are not all in one plane.
- A triangle mesh needs at least one triangle, and every index must name a vertex.
- A heightfield of `n × n` samples needs exactly `n * n` finite heights, with `n >= 2`.
- No shape may be larger than 2 km along any axis.
