# RE5016: Invalid step length or query

A physics step length or a query's arguments were out of range.

## How to fix it

- Step with the fixed time step, between 0.000001 s and 1 s (1/60 s by default).
- Query origins and centres must be finite; directions must not be zero; distances and
  radii must be positive.
- Shape casts take boxes, spheres, capsules and convex hulls, never meshes or
  heightfields.
