# RE5016: Invalid step length or query

A physics step length, a query's arguments or a move of the simulation origin
were out of range.

## Example

```text
error[RE5016]: step length 2 s is outside 0.000001..=1 s
  = hint: step with the fixed time step (1/60 s by default)
```

## How to fix it

- Step with the fixed time step, between 0.000001 s and 1 s (1/60 s by default).
- Query origins and centres must be finite; directions must not be zero; distances and
  radii must be positive.
- Shape casts take boxes, spheres, capsules and convex hulls, never meshes or
  heightfields.
- `Physics::set_origin` (world streaming's floating origin, ADR-0042) takes a
  finite point no more than 10 km from the current origin per axis.
