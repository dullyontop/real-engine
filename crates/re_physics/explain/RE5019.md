# RE5019: Invalid ragdoll

A ragdoll could not be created, posed, switched or pushed (ADR-0040). The message names the
problem, and the command was dropped: nothing was created or changed.

## How to fix it

- The skeleton needs 1 to 1024 joints, with joint 0 the only root, every parent before its
  children, and unique names. `re_app` builds it from the skin, under one added root joint,
  so a skin with several roots works.
- Every part names a different joint of the skeleton, and one part must be above all the
  others (the root part, usually the hips or pelvis).
- Masses and motor frequencies must be positive, friction not negative, the twist and plane
  axes non-zero and not parallel. Limits are finite degrees within ±180° (a swing cone from
  0 to 180°), each minimum at most its maximum.
- Part shapes are boxes, spheres, capsules or convex hulls: meshes and heightfields are
  static only.
- A pose has one finite transform per skeleton joint, inside the world limits.
- A powered strength is from 0 to 1.
- Impulses need a simulated or powered ragdoll; an animated one follows its animation.
- An entity has a ragdoll instead of a body or character, not both.
