# RE8807: A generated entity was left out of a copy

A procedural source makes its entities, and some of them show a mesh or a
heightfield the source made too (an asset named `derived:` and a hash). That
asset exists only while the source makes it, so a copy of such an entity
would show nothing. The editor leaves it out of the copy, with this note,
and copies the rest.

## Example

```text
note[RE8807]: terrain/chunk_3_4 was not copied: it shows `derived:4f1c…`, which exists only while its procedural source makes it
  = hint: bake the source (`proc.bake`) to copy what it made
```

## How to fix it

- Bake the source (`proc.bake`): its output becomes ordinary entities with
  their meshes written to files, and those copy like any other entity.
- Or copy the procedural source itself: the copy keeps its graph, parameters
  and overrides, and generates its own output.

Generated entities without derived assets (a placed prefab, a light) are
copied as ordinary entities.
