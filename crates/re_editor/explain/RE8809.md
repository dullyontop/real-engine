# RE8809: The Hierarchy did not move an entity

Dragging rows in the Hierarchy moves entities: onto a row puts them under
that entity, between two rows puts them beside it, and under the rows puts
them at the scene's root. Some moves cannot be made, and the editor leaves
those entities where they are, with this note:

- an entity cannot go under itself or one of its own descendants;
- an entity a procedural source made moves only within that source's
  output (under the source or another of its entities), where `proc.move`
  keeps the move each time the source generates again;
- an authored entity cannot go under a generated one, which the source
  replaces each time it generates.

The other dragged entities still move.

## Example

```text
note[RE8809]: not moved: town/house_3 is made by town, so it moves only within town's output
  = hint: drop it on town or on another of its entities, or bake town (`proc.bake`) to move what it made anywhere
```

## How to fix it

- Drop the entity somewhere it can go.
- To move what a source made anywhere in the scene, bake the source
  (`proc.bake`): its output becomes ordinary entities.
- To add an entity among a source's output, use `proc.insert`.
