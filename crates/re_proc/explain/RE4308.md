# RE4308: Floating origin cannot cover every focus

The simulation origin follows the stream focuses (ADR-0042). With several
focuses it sits on the `[stream] origin_grid` point nearest their mean. One
focus is too far from that point to stay within physics range (±5 km). Bodies
near it lose precision.

## Example

```text
warning[RE4308]: focus `player2` is 6.3 km from the origin [4096, 0, 0]; physics range is 5 km
```

## How to fix it

Keep the focuses closer together, or give the far one no `StreamFocus`
component, so that the world does not stream around it.
