# RE4309: Content beyond the precision limit

Scene positions are 32-bit floats in world space. Within ±16 km they are
exact to about 2 mm. Content farther out loses precision: it jitters, and
seams open between pieces. Streamed worlds are supported up to that size.

## Example

```text
warning[RE4309]: cell c/70_0 lies 17.9 km from the world origin, beyond the 16 km precision limit
```

## How to fix it

Keep the world within 16 km of its origin, for example with the streamed
source's `Streamed.bounds`.
