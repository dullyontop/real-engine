# RE4023: Structural override refused

`proc.move`, `proc.replace`, `proc.insert` or `proc.clear_structure` could
not change a procedural source's structural overrides (ADR-0043 §3).
Nothing changed. The message says why: the entity is not generated (or not a
procedural source), its source is streamed, the new parent is not the source
or an entity of its output, the move would put an entity under itself, the
insert key is taken or not a plain name, or the replacement does not turn
into entities (an unknown component or field, or a prefab that cannot be
read or has more than one root).

## Example

```text
error[RE4023]: the parent `rocks/3` is not `forest` or an entity of its output
```

## How to fix it

Pass a generated entity's id (`<source>/<key>`) to `proc.move` and
`proc.replace`, and the source's id to `proc.insert`. Pick a parent from the
same source's output. Give a prefab with one root entity, or components the
registry knows. To change an existing insert, clear it with
`proc.clear_structure {source, key: "+<key>"}` first.
