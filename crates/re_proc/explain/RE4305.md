# RE4305: Cell applied late

A cell's content was requested at tick `t`, but was not ready at its apply
tick `t + delay_ticks`. In play it is applied when it arrives, so the frame
does not wait, and this warning is logged once per cell. In `real verify`,
the D2 harness and replays the frame waits for it instead, and the report
counts these waits. That keeps `state_hash` the same on fast and slow machines.

## Example

```text
warning[RE4305]: cell c/12_4 was applied 6 ticks late (generation took longer than delay_ticks)
```

## How to fix it

Raise `[stream] delay_ticks`, or make the cell cheaper to generate: fewer
points scattered, a coarser spacing, less erosion. On a machine with more
cores, raise `[stream] workers`.
