# RE4016: Budget exceeded

A budgeted evaluation (`graph.generate`, which runs on a copy of the world)
took longer than its time budget or produced more entities than its entity
budget, and was stopped. Nothing in the project changed.

## Example

```text
error[RE4016]: evaluation of `island` stopped after 2003 ms (budget 2000 ms) before node `scatter`
```

## How to fix it

Raise `max_ms` or `max_entities`, or reduce the work: a smaller `size` on
field nodes, a larger `radius` on `scatter.poisson`.
