# RE3101: Scene request refused

A scene request does not fit the scenes that are loaded or asked for:

- `scene.load` of a scene that is already loaded, or already asked for;
- `scene.unload` of the main scene, or of a scene that is not loaded;
- `scene.keep` of an entity that is not a root (it has a parent), or of a
  streamed entity, which belongs to the main scene.

## Example

```text
error[RE3101]: scene.unload: "town" is the main scene; switch to another scene instead
```

## How to fix it

Read `scene.current()` and `scene.loaded()` before asking. To replace the main
scene, use `scene.switch`; `scene.load` and `scene.unload` are for scenes added
beside it. Keep the root of what should survive a switch: its subtree goes with
it.
