# RE4501: Invalid primitive

A mesh primitive (box, sphere, cylinder, capsule or plane) was asked for with
a size or tessellation it cannot be built with:

- sizes (half extents, radius, half height, plane size) must be finite and
  positive (a capsule's half height may be zero);
- a sphere, cylinder or capsule needs at least 3 segments around and a sphere
  or capsule at least 2 rings; a plane needs at least 1 subdivision;
- tessellation is capped at 1024 segments and rings per primitive.

## Example

```text
error[RE4501]: invalid sphere: radius must be finite and positive, got -1
```

## How to fix it

Use positive sizes and a segment count of 3 or more. In a ProcGraph, these
come from the `mesh.primitive` node's parameters.
