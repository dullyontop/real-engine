# RE4503: Polygon out of range

A polygon, path or road has a point too far from its source's origin.
Polygon operations snap every point to a 1 mm grid of 32-bit integers, which
reaches about 1,000 km in each direction.

## Example

```text
error[RE4503]: out of range: shape 0 has the point [2000000.0, 0.0], beyond ±1000000 m
```

## How to fix it

Keep shapes and roads within about 1,000 km of the procedural source. A
larger world is split into several sources (or streamed cells), each with
its own origin.
