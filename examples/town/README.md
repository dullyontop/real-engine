# Town

Town Courier, a sample game that exercises the engine from end to end: a town
generated by the project's own Luau node kinds and the engine's nodes, a truck
on Jolt's vehicle model, gameplay in scripts (deliveries, parcels that fall off
the truck, an autopilot that plans routes through the streets), a mixer with
snapshots, and a HUD. CI checks it in the `verify` job, and packs it and
compares the packaged game with the project in `build-package`.

Deliver parcels around the town before the shift ends. Stop in the depot's
loading bay to load them; each is for an address marked by a yellow beacon.
Stop at the kerb by the address, slowly, to deliver: 100 points a parcel, up
to 150 more for a quick delivery and 50 for one that never fell off. A hard
knock throws parcels off the truck: stop next to one to pick it up, or it is
lost after 25 s. With the bed empty, the depot (a green beacon) has more.
When the time is up the results show, and Enter builds a new town for the
next shift.

W and S (or the left stick's Y) are throttle and brake: against the way the
truck rolls they brake first, then reverse or drive on. A and D steer, Space
is the hand brake, Enter starts a shift, P (or Escape) pauses it, C switches
between the chase camera and the view from above, R puts the truck back on
its wheels and H sounds the horn. `real.toml` maps the gamepad too.

```sh
real run --project examples/town
real verify --project examples/town
real check --types --project examples/town
real build --project examples/town --out dist/town
```

The scene's `game` entity holds the rules (`Rules`): the shift's length
(`shift_seconds`, 240), the parcels loaded at a time (`parcels`, 5), and
`autopilot`, which lets the autopilot work the shift, as the title screen's
does while it drives round the town.

`real.toml` maps the input, turns on mild post-processing (neutral tone
mapping, a little bloom on the lamps and slightly richer colour), sets the
sun's shadow cascades (four, out to 220 m), names the packaged game
(`com.example.town`) and packs `models/`, which is not a default package
folder. The mixer (`[audio]`, ADR-0041) has buses for the effects, the
truck's engine (under `sfx`, through a compressor), the HUD's sounds, the
music and the town's ambience; everything under `sfx` also feeds `street`, a
short reverb send. The `paused` snapshot muffles the music and turns the world
down while a shift is paused, and `hurry` brings the music up for the shift's
last quarter (at most half a minute).

## Layout

- `scripts/tools/town_graph.luau`: the town's ProcGraph (ADR-0015), built by
  a script (the town entity's `GraphSource.builder`). `project.town_plan`
  lays out a grid of junctions, five by five and 64 m apart, the inner ones
  moved by the seed so the inner streets bend, and decides what each block is
  for (houses, a park or the depot). `project.street` turns each row and
  column of junctions into a road path. The engine's nodes make the roads
  (`road.mesh`), their footprint and the pavements (`road.footprint`,
  `shape.boolean`), and the ground (`terrain.conform`, the hills flattened
  under the town). `project.town` builds the houses and shops with their
  addresses, the depot and the markers the game finds its way by, and
  `project.greenery` places the trees, bushes and lamps (`points.instance`).
- `scripts/generators/`: the Luau node kinds (ADR-0043 §4), with their
  modules in `lib/` (the shared plan and street curve, polygons, buildings
  and a small mesh builder). They are checked against the generator API
  (`.real/types/generator.d.luau`), not the game's.
- `scenes/main.scene.json`: the town source (`Procedural`, seed 1), the truck
  (a dynamic `Vehicle` with four wheels and one box collider), its three
  engine sound layers, the camera, the sun, the HUD's lines, the music, the
  ambience and the `game` entity. Its `script_components` declare `Rules` and
  the markers `project.town` leaves: `Address`, `Depot`, `SpawnPoint` and
  `Junction`.
- `scripts/game/game.luau`: the game: the title, shift and results screens,
  the parcels' jobs and their beacons, delivery and scoring, the HUD, the
  pause and a new town after each shift (`proc.regenerate` with the next
  seed). `truck.luau` drives the truck (`physics.drive`) from the player's
  input or the autopilot, measures the knocks that throw parcels off, sounds
  the horn and the crashes, resets the truck and plays its engine: three
  layers crossfaded by rpm and pitched to it. `camera.luau` is the chase,
  overhead and orbit cameras; the chase and orbit cameras move in when a
  house is in the way.
- `scripts/game/lib/`: `town` reads the markers of the generated town,
  `route` plans the way along the streets (Dijkstra over the junctions, along
  each street's spline), `autopilot` follows a route (pure pursuit, slowing
  for the bends ahead, turning round in the street when the route is
  behind), `cargo` keeps the parcels on the truck's bed and the ones thrown
  off, `state` is what the scripts share, and `util` has small helpers.
- `models/truck.glb` and `models/wheel.glb`: the truck, a pickup with an open
  bed, and its wheel, in vertex colours.
- `assets/`: the sounds: the engine's three layers, the horn, crashes, a
  parcel landing, loading, delivery, a lost parcel, the shift's whistle and
  bell, the music and the town's ambience.
- `tools/make_models.py` and `tools/make_sounds.py` write the models and the
  sounds (standard-library Python, the same bytes on every run); run them
  from this directory after changing them.
- `tests/replays/`: `drive` (the player drives from the depot to the first
  address and delivers, then looks from above, pauses and carries on, sounds
  the horn and resets the truck), `shift` (the autopilot works a whole
  60 s shift, to the results) and `newtown` (a 10 s shift, then Enter on the
  results builds a new town, seed 2, and starts a shift in it). `real verify`
  runs each twice with the same `state_hash` and checks goldens for Vulkan
  (lavapipe) and GL; there are no DX12 or Metal goldens yet.

A script callback may run for 4 ms of wall time (`[scripts] cpu_budget_ms`),
so the scripts spread the big jobs over steps: reading a new town's markers
(`town.refresh`) and laying out its streets (`route.prepare`) wait for the
next step after each batch.

Everything here is made by the engine's generators or by the scripts in
`tools/`; there are no third-party assets.
